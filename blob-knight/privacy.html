Blob Knight

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 6 August 2026

English

Blob Knight is designed to work offline. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

The app does not include user accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The developer does not collect personal data from the app.

Blob Knight uses Apple StoreKit to retrieve localized product information and to process or restore the optional Full Game Unlock purchase. Purchase processing is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive your payment details.

If you voluntarily open a support request on GitHub, the information you submit is processed by GitHub under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not include purchase receipts or other sensitive information.

For privacy questions, use the support link published on the App Store product page.
